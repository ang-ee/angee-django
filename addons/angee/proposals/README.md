# Proposals

- The [project setup contributor](models.py) composes `Round.objects.provision` after milestones and bindings; `RoundTemplate` owns dates and topic keys, and admission owns responders and tracks.
- The [question projections](schema.py) count readable unanswered recipients through the same predicate as `clarification_waiting`. Inherited question visibility reads through the parent task's current ReBAC policy.
- A question's `comment_withheld` arm removes widened readers from the base task and queue post grants. Its readable managers, asker and current assignee may comment; for a manager's passed question addressed to all responders, each current responder may comment. The current assignee includes a requester after a pass. Ordinary task comments keep their native grants.
- [`Round.open`](models.py) stamps disclosure once for a manager in any lifecycle state, after the round's phase has come: with `opens_after` set, the target project's current phase must follow that milestone (`Round.opening_phase_condition`). A collecting round becomes opened; a canceled round keeps its terminal state. `can_open` reports the same gate, is true for a manager only until that receipt exists, and readers see `opened_at`.
- [`Round.objects.resolve_clarification`](models.py) owns single and batch resolution. Each selected question needs management authority, an observed revision and an eligible state; batches use [work's partial-success contract](../work/README.md).
- The [record contributions](web/src/record-rounds.tsx) place ceremonies, People and Approach on Round and Project records. The [active-round selector](models.py) picks the newest readable collecting or opened round from the project, source Task or proposal track; historical rounds remain in the Rounds tab.
- [Answer declarations](web/src/views/ProposalsPage.tsx) bind the [shared visibility widget](../../../packages/ui/src/widgets/visibility.tsx) to server choices and the revision-checked verb. The [comparison grid](web/src/comparison-grid.tsx) follows the server's readable proposal columns, keeps topic order, and edits the responder's permitted answer inside its cell through the resource create/update path. Every answer shows its audience; a manager's chip uses the revision-checked audience verb. The grid omits offer facts unless its host requests them with `facts`. Its column and fact labels come from proposals vocabulary; hosts can supply the section heading, hint and audience.
- [ProposalStatements](web/src/proposal-statements.tsx) is the separate statement presentation for a host tab. It receives the same readable proposal rows, renders one card per row, and saves the responder's own statement with the proposal revision. Hosts supply section headings through props and composer wording through proposals vocabulary. The [comparison read](web/src/comparison-data.ts) projects the permissions and revisions used by both surfaces; its server result determines disclosure.

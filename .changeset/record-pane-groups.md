Every record pane reads the record through one wrap (`FormViewPane`); pane
groups follow the saved-record tabs and render inside the record chrome
(`FormViewPaneGroup`). Record edge tabs show only on types that can carry their
edge, read from the type's `recordEdges` metadata. Tags are a placeable field
on the owner's form and list.

The expanded rail reorders apps and picks the default app like the icon rail,
sharing one persisted order (`RailSortable`), and the logo expands the rail. An
app root or a deployment app may set `railExpanded`. After sign-in `/` lands on
the person's home, then the rail default, the product's home, then the first
app in their rail. Leaving Settings keeps the shell and returns to the previous
place (`ChromePlaceProvider`, `useChromePlace`).

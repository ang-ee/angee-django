## How to sign

This agreement covers Contributions to **all repositories in the `ang-ee` GitHub
organization**.

A Corporate CLA is executed out of band, not through the pull request bot. Send a
signed copy of this agreement, together with the initial list of designated
contributor GitHub accounts, to **legal@angee.ai**. Once countersigned, your
designated accounts are added to the signature register and their pull requests
pass the CLA check automatically.
